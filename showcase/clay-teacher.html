01 · Clay

What I see you building

Clay coined the term GTM Engineer for people who run growth agents the way software engineers run coding agents. It is funding a $1M scholarship program to train them, and Clay University is the core IP of that GTME ecosystem.

This teacher is a main on-camera presenter for Clay University: courses, Looms, walkthroughs and informal live builds, created with Solutions, GTMOps and Forward Deployed GTMEs, published fast and re-recorded as AI tools change.

GTM Engineering is a new discipline with no content playbook, and the tools change monthly. Clay needs a teacher who learns fast, builds on camera and ships lessons at the pace the field moves.
