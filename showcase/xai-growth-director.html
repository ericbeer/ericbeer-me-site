01 · xAI

What I see you building

xAI, now SpaceXAI, runs the X ads business and is building ads into Grok, which it pitches to advertisers as a high-intent placement. Its stated goal is for Grok to eventually automate much of the ad-buying process.

This director owns growth marketing for the Ads business and leads the insights, measurement and analytics function. The job is to turn ad performance data into recommendations that grow advertiser revenue, and to build the playbooks, training and case studies sales teams use.

It works with sales, product, engineering and the Head of Global Advertising, sets KPIs for advertiser health and revenue impact, and represents the company with advertisers, agencies and partners.

X needs to win back and grow advertiser spend, and that depends on measurement advertisers trust and sales teams who can show what works.
