01 · OpenAI

What I see you building

ChatGPT now reaches more than a billion people each week, and creators, experts and educators are a big part of how people learn what it can do. OpenAI's Creator Partnerships team, inside Partnerships, builds trusted relationships with them through product education, partner feedback and collaborative programs.

This Strategic Partner Manager owns a portfolio of strategic partners: originating relationships, understanding each partner's audience and business, designing partnership plans, and leading deals from first conversation through negotiation, execution and growth.

The role also carries judgment. It coordinates with Product, Marketing, Communications, Global Affairs, Research, Legal and Safety, represents partners internally, and weighs each opportunity for alignment, feasibility, impact and risk, including rights, safety and reputation.

OpenAI needs creators and experts to show the world valuable, trustworthy uses of AI, and that takes partnerships built on real understanding of each partner's audience, incentives and concerns.
