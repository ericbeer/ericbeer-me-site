What I see at Gametime

Four things from Gametime's own pages and public reports that shape how I'd run web growth.

+90 MIN

You can buy after the game starts

Tickets stay on sale up to 90 minutes after an event begins, and prices often fall as start time approaches. The whole product is built for urgency, so web pages have to load fast and convert in a few taps.

110%

Price shown is price paid

Gametime shows fees in the price up front, and its Ticket Coverage promises the lowest price or 110% of the difference (restrictions apply). That is a real message advantage in paid search, where people compare prices.

60+

A resale marketplace across North America

Sports, concerts, theater and comedy, from inventory listed by resellers, in 60+ cities by public reports. Every team, artist and venue page is a long-tail search entry that SEO can win, and paid search is paying for today.

$91.5M

Venture-backed, lean growth team

About $91.5M raised by public reports, Accel among the backers, latest round $30M in 2022. Growth is a small team of channel owners. The VP Growth already has paid social, CRM and brand covered. Web is the open seat.

Sources: gametime.co, the Gametime App Store listing, the Built In posting and public funding reports.

The brief

What the posting asks for, what it pays, and the problem underneath it.

Asking for
  • One owner for web-driven GMV and blended contribution economics, not one channel's KPIs.
  • Run the paid search team: strategy, budget, performance.
  • Work with Product on CRO and an experimentation culture across landing pages and flows.
  • Build SEO and content from zero, likely through agencies or contractors.
  • A data-backed view on web-to-app: when web should drive installs and when it should close the sale itself.
  • Dashboards, metrics and business reviews. Reports to the VP of Growth.
Offering

$240K–$270K + equity, remote in the US. Flexible PTO, $1,200 a year in Gametime event credits, medical, dental and vision, life and disability, fertility benefits through Carrot, 401k with HSA and pre-tax savings, company off-sites and wellness programs.

The problem

Right now web performance is split across channels and nobody owns the total. Paid search spends against tonight's demand, SEO doesn't exist yet, and CRO sits with Product. Gametime needs one person accountable for web GMV and what each dollar of it costs, who can also decide which web visitors should go to the app.

Requirement → proof

Each requirement in the posting, how I'd approach it, and what I've actually done. Where I'm lighter, I say so.

SEAT01Owned a revenue number

How I think

Owning GMV means owning the total, so I'd rather cut a good-looking channel than defend it. One P&L view, reviewed every week.

Proof

Founder & CEO of Universal Marketing Partners from 2008 to about 2022, an 8-figure performance and affiliate business where revenue was the only scoreboard. Teams from 5–10 up to 15–20.

SEAT02Full-funnel economics

How I think

CPC, CPA, AOV, take rate, refunds, LTV, payback. I'm a math guy. A $40 CPA can be great on a playoff ticket and a loss on a Tuesday-night game, so bids should follow contribution margin, not blended averages.

Proof

At LeadQual.ai I buy and sell live insurance leads, where margin is set lead by lead. Earlier I scaled a language-learning product from under $10M to over $100M market cap with Facebook arbitrage, which only works when you know your margin per click.

SEAT03Paid search / SEM

How I think

I'd run the paid search team on economics: margin-based bid targets by event type and days to event, brand vs. non-brand kept separate, and all-in pricing in the ad copy.

Proof

I've led media buyers for 20+ years. I was agency of record for RealAge.com (Hearst / Dr. Oz) and Harlequin Book Club, and drove customers for LifeLock, Norton Antivirus and Credit Sesame.

To be straight: paid search is some of my work, not the core. I lead the people at the keyboard and set the math they bid to. I'm not the hands-on SEM specialist.

SEAT04Web product sense & CRO

How I think

Find where the funnel leaks: event page → seat map → checkout. Then fix the biggest leak first, with a test plan Product can actually ship. Direct response taught me to test the offer before the button color.

Proof

I've shipped 50+ apps and AI tools, plus the funnels behind them. I built BeChosen / Scout, an AI job platform with membership tiers, and the consent-compliant lead flows at LeadQual.ai.

SEAT05Built a channel from zero

How I think

SEO from zero is mostly vendor management: choose the right agency, give them a page plan (teams, venues, artists, "tickets tonight") and judge them on attributed GMV, not traffic.

Proof

I launched JVC Affiliate Network on Everflow in 2025 with 280+ affiliates now tracked, built AffiliateSchool.ai (17 digital products and a 5-level partner plan), and started BeChosen's creator-led acquisition program.

SEAT06Judged on outcomes

How I think

Channel KPIs are useful for diagnosing problems. Business outcomes are how you get paid. I'd put the web team's work in terms the CFO uses.

Proof

$100M+ in affiliate commissions generated over my career, all of it paid only on results. Memberships for Disney Book Club, and customers for brands that measured every acquisition.

The 90-day plan, in nine innings

Each inning is about 10 days. The first third is learning the numbers, the second is fixing the biggest leaks, and the last third is building something that compounds.

Read the field

DAYS 1–30
1

One GMV truth

Agree with Data and Finance on web GMV, contribution margin and attribution, including web sessions that end in an app purchase.

2

Meet the paid search team

Audit the account by event category and days to event. Find where spend runs ahead of margin and move it.

3

Funnel walk

Map event page → seat map → checkout on mobile web with Product. Rank the leaks by GMV at stake and agree on the first three tests.

Fix the leaks

DAYS 31–60
4

Margin-based bidding

Move paid search to contribution targets by segment. Put all-in pricing and the 110% guarantee in the copy and test it.

5

Test cadence

Get a weekly test rhythm going with a shared backlog, pre-registered metrics and a readout everyone trusts.

6

SEO kickoff

Choose the agency or contractors. Ship the first set of team, venue and "tickets tonight" pages, judged on GMV per page.

Build what compounds

DAYS 61–90
7

Web-to-app rule

Use cohort data to decide which visitors get pushed to install and which should check out on web. Write it down and test it.

8

Cross-channel input

Work with paid social and CRM on the landing pages and retargeting that feed the web funnel, without taking over their channels.

9

First business review

Present web GMV, contribution margin and test wins in one view, plus a 2027 plan with targets I'm willing to be judged on.

FINAL

By day 90: one agreed web GMV number, paid search bidding to margin, a weekly test cadence, SEO live and producing, and a written web-to-app rule. AI handles the busywork, like ad copy variants, page briefs and test readouts, so the team spends its time on decisions.
