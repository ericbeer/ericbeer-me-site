01 / What I see at NMT04

I know the funnel, and the trust problem.

The business

Onchain wealth management where the client keeps the keys.

Self-custodied smart accounts on Safe, with NMT managing strategy on a discretionary basis. Contracts audited by Consensys Diligence. Open to new clients since August 2026.

The customer

Investors putting $100,000 or more into a new idea.

They're interested in onchain yield and wary of custody risk. They need to understand the model, trust the people and believe the numbers before they book a call.

The market

A trust-driven category where one misleading claim costs everything.

Crypto marketing is loud and often careless. The firm that wins this audience sounds like a wealth manager, proves its claims and stays compliant.

The model

High-ticket, call-based: paid traffic and content into booked calls.

That makes funnel economics the whole game: cost per booked call, show rate, close rate, account size and payback, with sales and marketing reading one scorecard.

My thesis for NMT

With a $100K minimum, trust is the conversion rate. Brand and direct response are one job.

04 / What I'd do90 days

The first 90 days.

Days 1–30

Find the leaks

  • Cost per booked call, show rate, close rate and account size by channel and creative
  • Listen to sales calls: the top objections and where trust breaks
  • Audit every claim on the site, ads and VSL with compliance
  • Meet each team member; set metric owners and a weekly cadence

Days 31–60

Fix the funnel

  • Cut spend that doesn't fund accounts; scale what does
  • Qualification and confirmation flows to lift show rate
  • New creative and landing pages built on the top objections
  • Launch a weekly founder-voice content series

Days 61–90

Scale to plan

  • Open borrowed-audience and partner channels
  • A monthly path from $500K to $2M with spend and payback by channel
  • Brand standards across site, content and PR
  • Hiring plan for the gaps the scorecard shows

A plan, not a result: it gets sharpened in the first conversations with your team.
