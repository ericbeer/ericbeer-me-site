01 / What I see at your insurer04

I know the business, and why innovation stalls in it.

The business

Life, health and property & casualty, under one roof.

Three product lines, three risk models and three sets of rules. Innovation here has to fit enterprise strategy and the risk framework, or it never leaves the lab.

The customer

Policyholders reached through agents, banks and digital.

Agencies, bancassurance and digital platforms each see a different customer. The best new products make it easier for every one of those channels to sell and serve.

The market

AI, embedded insurance and new reporting rules, all at once.

IFRS 17 is changing how results are measured, AI is changing how customers are found and served, and embedded insurance is moving the point of sale into other companies' products.

The model

Distribution-led, compliance-bound, data-rich.

That's the world I already work in: regulated lead generation, consent and compliance, and partner economics where every dollar is tracked.

My thesis for your insurer

Innovation isn't a lab. It's a pipeline from idea to pilot to revenue, with a kill date, a metric and a compliance check at every step.
