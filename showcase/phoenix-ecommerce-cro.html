01 / What I see at Phoenix04

I know your platform, and your merchant.

The business

An autonomous commerce platform: storefront, checkout, payments and ops, with AI agents doing the work.

Founded in 2024 and already at 8-figure ARR growing over 100% a year. That's product-market fit. The CRO's job is turning it into a repeatable, multi-segment revenue machine.

The customer

DTC and direct-response founders who live on conversion rate, approval rate and back-end LTV.

These are operators who know their CPA to the penny. They don't respond to generic SaaS pitches. They respond to someone who can show them more money on the same traffic.

The market

Direct response is about a third of ecommerce transactions, underserved by platforms built for brand retail.

The winner owns the trust of the DR ecosystem: the agencies, affiliate networks, payment partners and media buyers who whisper which stack to use.

The model

Series A, high growth, a lean remote team building an AI-native GTM.

So the revenue org has to be designed, not just hired: partners as a channel, marketing that targets founders by name, and AI doing the heavy lifting in sales and RevOps.

My thesis for Phoenix

Phoenix wins DR founders the way DR founders win customers: through partners paid on results, proof in the numbers and a relentless test-and-scale loop.
