01 · HeyGen

What I see you building

HeyGen makes AI video: avatars, translation and generated video that let anyone produce studio-quality content without cameras. It serves creators, marketers and businesses directly and offers an API so other companies can build HeyGen video into their own products.

Growth now depends on distribution as much as direct signups: native integrations with social, creative and productivity platforms, OEM and embedded deals, white-label licensing and revenue-share partnerships, plus a scalable program for API partners.

This is the founding leader of partnerships, reporting to the CMO. It starts as a hands-on dealmaker running deals from sourcing to post-launch, with the option to build a team as the function grows.

HeyGen needs a partnerships function that puts its video into the platforms where people already create and work, with deal structures that protect its economics and keep partners growing usage after launch.
