01 · Anthropic

What I see you building

Anthropic launched the Claude Partner Network in March 2026 with an initial $100M for partner training, enablement and joint market development, and plans to grow its partner-facing team fivefold. Partners get a portal, Anthropic Academy training, sales playbooks, co-marketing documents and a growing certification path.

The Reseller track is where authorized partners sell Claude directly to end customers. This is the first dedicated partner marketing role for that track, global in scope, working across Partnerships, Commercial Operations, Channel Account Managers, Partner Operations, Customer Marketing and Communications.

The job is to make the channel a repeatable revenue engine: a playbook and standards, MDF that ties to pipeline, localized campaign kits, customer stories timed to tier reviews, and KPIs that show which partners activate in 30, 60 and 90 days.

Anthropic needs resellers to generate predictable, measurable Claude pipeline, and today there is no dedicated marketing owner turning program benefits, MDF and co-marketing into partner revenue.
