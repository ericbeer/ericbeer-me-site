Section A · What I see at Campione LawPage A1

A strong firm with a scattered marketing story.

Four things stood out when I studied the firm, its offices and the role.

The heritage

A Jacksonville firm with over $300 million recovered.

Personal injury, workers' comp, criminal defense, real estate and business litigation. Verdicts and settlements in the millions, and a brand built on "Integrity, Compassion, Results."

The customer

Injured people, hurt workers and people facing charges.

They want someone they can reach. Campione answers that with free consultations, home visits and the attorney's own cell number, in English, Spanish and Portuguese.

The market

Already reaching well past the home office.

Offices and satellite offices from Jacksonville to Orlando, Tampa and Miami, plus Savannah, Las Vegas and Newport Beach. Each one is a market with its own media, costs and competitors.

The model

No up-front fee means the firm funds every case it wins.

Every billboard, radio spot and lead vendor is paid for before a case signs. So the number that matters is cost per signed case, by channel and by market.

My thesis

Campione Law doesn't need more channels. It needs one engine: every channel, vendor and market scored on cost per signed case, then expanded where the math works.
