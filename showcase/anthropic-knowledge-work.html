01 · Anthropic

What I see you building

Claude for Knowledge Work is Anthropic's push to make Claude the tool knowledge workers use every day. It covers Claude chat, Cowork (Claude's agent for files and tasks on the desktop), Claude for Office and Tag.

This lead sets GTM strategy and integrated growth for that product line. The work means steering Demand Gen, Growth, Brand, Events, Campaigns, Field and Success without owning them, while writing plans, building campaigns and making content directly as an individual contributor in year one.

Success is measured in awareness, weekly active users and account depth, revenue contribution and campaign efficiency. The posting names influencer and creator work aimed at ops, finance, legal, marketing and productivity audiences.

Knowledge workers adopt tools by role, not by model release, and Anthropic needs one owner to turn Claude's knowledge-work products into segment plans, flagship stories and activation programs that build weekly habits inside accounts.
