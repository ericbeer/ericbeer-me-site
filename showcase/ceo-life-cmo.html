01 / What I see at CEO Life04

I know your business, and your buyer.

The model

Local chapters, national platform, partners with skin in the game.

35+ chapters run by Chapter Partners who invested real money. That's a franchise-style model: the corporate team wins only when each partner can see their own numbers improve.

The customer

CEOs, founders and C-suite leaders who don't answer cold ads.

I'm one of them. I've built and run my own companies for 20+ years. Executives join because of who else is in the room, so the message has to sell the room, not the event.

The market

Every metro has competing peer groups, masterminds and clubs.

Winning a market is local: the right audiences, the right referral sources, the right events. A national playbook with local tuning beats 35 separate experiments.

The funnel

Market → audience → lead → nurture → appointment → interview → member.

A membership funnel with an interview step. That's a lead-gen and qualification problem, and qualification is exactly what I build: I founded an AI lead-qualification company.

My thesis for CEO Life

Road to 20 isn't about more ad spend. It's about one scorecard, one playbook and a qualification engine that turns leads into appointments in every chapter.

02 / The brief03

What you asked for. What you're offering. The real job.

What you're asking for

  • A Road to 20 plan for each chapter
  • Full-funnel ownership, market to member
  • 6–10 qualified appointments per chapter per business day
  • Paid media oversight: Meta, Instagram, LinkedIn and new channels
  • Landing pages, application flows and nurture
  • Marketing Managers hired, trained and run on SOPs
  • Chapter Partner reporting and a central command center

What you're offering

  • Chief Marketing Officer, reporting to the CEO
  • $150K–$250K OTE, base plus variable
  • 100% remote, US
  • Target CAC of about $1,000–$1,200 per member
  • About $20K–$24K marketing budget per chapter per month
  • A 90-day ramp standard for new chapters

The problem to solve

Produce 20 new members a month in every chapter at a CAC partners trust.

That means a funnel built backward from 20, appointments booked and shown, creative that's found and scaled, managers running a shared playbook, and a dashboard that tells every partner the truth weekly.
