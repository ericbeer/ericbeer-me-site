What I see at KREWE

An independent house with a point of view, three ways to buy, and a site that now has to lead.

Born on a bike cart in 2013

Stirling Barrett sold the first frames from a cart at New Orleans festivals. KREWE still makes small-batch, hand-crafted sun and optical frames in plant-based acetates and premium metals, backed by Second Chances replacement.

18 stores and a table

Maisons du KREWE at 619 Royal and 67 Gansevoort, plus Houston, Charleston, Nashville, Dallas and more, with PATULA bistro and wine bar in the mix.

Three channels

Online, its own stores and wholesale through 500+ retailers such as Bloomingdale's and Nordstrom. The founder once described the business as roughly thirds.

Scale, and a stack that's ready

Third-party estimates put KREWE near $100M in revenue with about 340 people. Public tech-detection shows Shopify, Klaviyo, Attentive and Triple Whale already in place. So this role isn't about buying tools. It's about owning the P&L, the team and the attribution, and growing digital 2X over three years, as the posting asks.

Sources: krewe.com/about, Glossy (2017), the job posting. Revenue, headcount and retailer counts are estimates from public business data.

The first collection

Ninety days in three looks: learn the house, set the numbers, then ship the season.

The runway

  • Present the three-year plan to 2X the channel, with the forecast behind it
  • Relaunch affiliate and creators on paid-on-performance terms
  • Tie store traffic and online demand into one weekly view with Retail
  • Give leadership a KPI deck they can read in five minutes
