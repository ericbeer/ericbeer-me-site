What I see at Bulletproof

A category-creating brand mid-reinvention, with a DTC site that has to stay profitable while retail and the new positioning do their work.

1

The brand that made butter coffee a habit

Founded in Seattle by Dave Asprey, Bulletproof turned coffee plus MCT oil into a daily ritual, then built a range around it: coffee, MCT oil, creamers, collagen and protein. Rituals are subscriptions waiting to happen.

2

Now part of a family of Americas brands

In 2024 Bulletproof became part of Bia Foods, alongside Badia, El Rey and Iberia Foods, backed by Guatemala's Grupo Mariposa. That means a parent that thinks in margin and distribution, not just growth at any cost.

3

A rebrand toward everyday wellness

The June 2025 refresh, "Coffee that lifts you up," moved Bulletproof from biohacking toward accessible wellness. A broader promise means new customers, and new customers need proof the economics hold.

4

DTC sits next to big retail

The bags sell on Amazon/Whole Foods, Sprouts, Kroger and Target, and on a Shopify Plus site run with Klaviyo and Triple Whale by public tech data. Bulletproof.com has to earn its place with subscribers, bundles and margin retail can't match.

Sources: Daily Coffee News (June 2025), the job posting, and public company data. Revenue is about $35M by public estimates.

Ninety days, three bands

Built for an interim seat: keep things steady first, improve what's provable, then hand back a cleaner business.

30

Days 1–30: hold the line

No surprises for the returning leader or for Grupo Mariposa.

  • Rebuild the weekly scorecard: revenue, contribution margin, active subscribers, churn, CAC by channel, AOV
  • Meet the agency, re-confirm scope and SLAs, agree a ranked test queue

60

Small, provable wins on margin and retention.

  • Klaviyo replenishment flows timed to when the bag runs out
