The first ninety days

Built for a portfolio, not a single brand: learn each platform, build the shared bench, then make it the default for every acquisition.

I

Days 1 to 30

Listen and audit

  • Sit with each brand team at Wellmore, Raw Sugar, ARG and Primal Harvest.
  • Inventory every affiliate program, network, creator deal and press contact already in place.

  • Open a press calendar tied to launches and retail moments.
