The first 90 days

The final stretch of the line: learn the curves, run the first tests, then make the expansion call.

Day30

Learn the curves

  • Rebuild the scorecard per condition: CAC by channel, payback, contribution margin, refill retention by month
  • Trace the path from a drug-price search to a condition sign-up, and find the biggest drop-off
  • Sit with Clinical Ops, Legal and Compliance to learn what is allowed in each condition before touching a test
  • Map where Companion and the condition plans overlap or compete

Outcome: one shared view of which conditions make money, and why.

Day60

Run the first tests

  • Price and cadence tests: monthly against multi-month refills, with churn and cash read side by side
  • A Companion plus condition bundle offer, and a win-back program for lapsed subscribers
  • On-site placements that turn price-search traffic into condition offers, built with Product
  • One new compliant acquisition channel, such as vetted affiliate or creator partners, at a capped CAC

Outcome: first lift in payback or retention, measured by cohort.

Day90

Make the expansion call

  • A scorecard for the next condition: search demand on GoodRx, margin, clinical complexity, partner readiness
  • A recommendation to the VP and leadership on which condition to open next and what it should cost to launch
  • A weekly operating rhythm across Marketing, Product, Data, Finance and Clinical Ops
  • A 2027 plan with targets for plans, churn and contribution margin

Outcome: a growth roadmap leadership can fund with confidence.
