01What I see at Asset Living

A national operator with a regional-sized voice.

RM-01The heritage

Founded in 1986, now 455K+ units, 12K+ employees and 800+ clients in 40+ states.

A true third-party manager across multifamily, student, affordable, active adult, build-to-rent and single-family rental. Forty years of operating proof that the market hasn't fully heard yet.

RM-02The customer

Two buyers. Owners and investors pick the manager. Residents pick the home.

Institutional clients want returns, reporting and trust. Residents want the right place at the right price, found fast. Different journeys, both complex, both measurable.

RM-03The market

Third-party management is won on reputation, and reputation is now answered by AI.

When an owner asks a search engine or an AI assistant "who should manage my portfolio," Asset Living needs to be in the answer. That's SEO, PR, awards and thought leadership working as one system.

RM-04The model

Units under management are the revenue. Leases keep them full.

Every new mandate adds fee revenue; every lease at a lower cost proves the value to the owner. About 6 corporate marketers and nearly 100 property marketers carry both.

My thesis

Asset Living doesn't need louder marketing. It needs more mandates won and more leases signed per dollar, on one scorecard.

    How I think

    Proof

  3. R-03"KPI dashboards tied to business outcomes" and "connecting marketing to financial results."
