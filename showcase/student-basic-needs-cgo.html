What I see at the Coalition

A product that already works

Navvy screens students for SNAP and Medicaid eligibility in under 10 minutes, backed by a peer navigator network that takes the stigma out of asking, and policy work with student and administrator advocates.

Real reach, at real scale

A partner curve that's bending

Funders in, earned revenue next

Backed by the DRK Foundation and the Gates Foundation's AI for Economic Opportunity initiative, with the Walmart Foundation and Ben & Jerry's among past sponsors. This role owns the earned-revenue P&L that sits alongside those grants.
