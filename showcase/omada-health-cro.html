What I see at Omada Health

A clinical leader with a growth equation to solve.

What you do

Virtual-first care for chronic conditions, now a public company.

Human care teams, connected devices and AI for diabetes, prediabetes, hypertension, cholesterol, joint and muscle health, plus GLP-1 support. More than two million members served, listed on Nasdaq as OMDA.

The customer

Two customers: the one who pays and the one who joins.

Health plans, PBMs, employers and health systems sign the contract. The member, often at no cost to them, still has to choose to start, use the devices and keep going.

The market

Cardiometabolic care meets the GLP-1 moment.

Buyers want outcomes and cost control, not another point solution. Medicare and government are the next big doors, and policy shapes how fast they open.

The model

B2B contracts, member-level economics.

1,900+ organizations enrolled. Revenue follows covered lives that enroll and engage, so the growth plan has to manage contract wins and enrollment yield together.

Omada doesn't only need more covered lives. It needs more of every covered life to enroll and engage, and new channels that bring the next ones in.
