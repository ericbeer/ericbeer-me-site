01 · Vercel

What I see you building

Vercel is the AI Cloud: the platform behind Next.js, v0, the AI SDK (3M+ weekly downloads) and the AI Gateway. It raised a $300M Series F at a $9.3B valuation in late 2025 with 82% year-over-year growth, and v0 has been used by more than 3.5 million developers.

The Partner Team works toward mutual success for Vercel, its partners and customers across SIs, GSIs, ISV co-sell and product partners. This role reports to the VP of Partnerships.

The job owns the partner journey from acquisition to acceleration: eligibility, incentives, tiering and agreements; onboarding, training, certification and badging; KPIs and rules of engagement; co-sell plays; partner community and advisory board; and data-driven decisions about what to expand, simplify or retire.

As AI apps move to production on Vercel, the partner ecosystem needs one clear program framework that makes partners productive and shows which partner types drive revenue.
