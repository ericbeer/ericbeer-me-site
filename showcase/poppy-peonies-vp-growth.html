01 / What I see at Poppy & Peonies04

I know your brand, and the women who carry it.

The heritage

A founder-led brand since 2015, built on bags that actually organize life.

Natalie Dusome built Poppy & Peonies around a simple promise: stylish bags with pockets for everything. Vegan leather, PETA-approved, plastic-free shipping. The values are part of the product.

The customer

Busy women who want style and order, and love to show both off.

She films what's in her bag. She shares a find with her friends. That's not a demographic, it's a distribution channel waiting to be paid.

The market

Crowded fashion DTC where paid social got expensive and TikTok Shop rewrote discovery.

The brands winning now sell through creators and affiliates, then use paid to amplify what's already working. Most haven't built that engine yet.

The model

DTC with retention and ecommerce leaders already in place.

So the growth seat is about adding the next engine, not rebuilding the store: TikTok Shop, affiliates and paid working as one system with one scorecard.

My thesis for Poppy & Peonies

Poppy & Peonies doesn't need more ad spend. It needs thousands of creators and customers paid to show the bag, with paid media pouring fuel on the winners.
