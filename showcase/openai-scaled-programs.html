01 · OpenAI

What I see you building

ChatGPT Ads reached a $1B annualized run rate in under 200 days, and OpenAI opened self-serve Ads Manager to more advertisers in May 2026. Ads run in the Free and Go tiers across 40+ countries, most campaigns buy on cost-per-click or outcome-optimized bidding, and small and midsized businesses are already a material share of the business.

OpenAI has built a network of 50+ technology and measurement partners, and agencies, adtech providers and consultants help clients build campaigns and connect conversion data. The SMB Ads Marketing team helps small businesses discover the platform, succeed as advertisers and grow spend, working with Product, Ads Engineering, Data Science and Sales.

This role sets the global strategy for the agencies and partners that serve SMBs: recruitment, segmentation, tiers and incentives, certification and enablement, co-marketing toolkits, and pilots that get measured and then scaled or retired.

Self-serve opened the door to millions of small businesses, but most will only succeed with help from an agency or partner. OpenAI needs a scaled program that recruits, trains and rewards those partners so SMB advertisers start well and keep spending.
