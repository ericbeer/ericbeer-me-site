01 · OpenAI

What I see you building

ChatGPT Ads launched in 2026 and reached a reported $1 billion annualized run rate in under 200 days, with tens of thousands of advertisers across more than 40 countries. Ads run in the Free and Go tiers in front of more than a billion weekly users, mostly bought on cost-per-click or outcome-optimized bidding.

OpenAI opened Ads Manager to self-serve advertisers in May and is expanding it internationally, and small and midsized businesses are already a material share of the business. Building a full self-serve platform means the advertiser funnel itself is now a product to grow.

This role owns that funnel for SMBs: acquisition across paid, SEO and GEO, social, partnerships, referrals and product-led growth, and activation through sign-up, onboarding, campaign creation and first spend, working closely with Product and Engineering.

OpenAI has massive audience demand for its ad product but needs a repeatable system that brings small advertisers in, gets them to a first campaign that works, and keeps them spending, at a CAC that pays back.
