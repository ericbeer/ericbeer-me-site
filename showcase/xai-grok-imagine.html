01 · xAI

What I see you building

Grok Imagine is xAI's image and video generation product inside Grok, now in the SpaceXAI organization. Recent model releases have pushed it toward longer, more controllable video, which puts it in direct competition with other generative media tools for creators and studios.

The Imagine Product team is small, flat and hands-on. This lead owns creator and community communications, with the stated goal of moving the brand from talking about itself to making other people talk about it.

The work spans showcase content for owned channels, two influencer motions (reach and craft), relationships with production houses and studios, vendor management, competitive tracking and measurement of what drives adoption.

Grok Imagine needs to be seen as a working tool for creators and professionals, not a novelty, and the team needs a program where outside voices and real workflows supply the proof.
