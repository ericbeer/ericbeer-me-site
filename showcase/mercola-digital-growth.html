The first 90 days

Sow, grow, harvest.

A season-by-season plan for Mercola's app. It gets sharper in the first conversations with your team.

Days 1–30

Sow

Measure the soil

  • Map the funnel from newsletter reader and Market buyer to app install, trial and paid
  • Audit CAC, LTV and payback by channel and by cohort
  • Check attribution and set up the first holdout tests
  • Talk with new, loyal and lapsed members about why they joined and why some left

Days 31–60

Grow

Plant the tests

  • Launch a results-paid creator and affiliate program for the app, with brand-safe rules
  • Convert the existing audience first: email, site and Market buyers into trials
  • Ship onboarding and trial-to-paid experiments with product
  • Stand up one weekly growth scorecard everyone reads

Days 61–90

Harvest

Scale what compounds

  • Move budget to the channels that proved incremental at target CAC
  • Test subscription pricing, annual plans and Market bundles for members
  • Set the team shape across performance, partners, lifecycle and analytics
  • Deliver a 12-month plan toward millions of paying members
