01 · Anthropic

What I see you building

Anthropic builds Claude, and startups are one of the fastest ways new capability becomes real products. Claude for Startups launched in May and was expanded in October 2026 with API credits, a year of Claude Team seats, up to $45,000 in partner offers through the Claude Startup Stack, and access to Anthropic's applied AI teams. Several thousand companies are already in it.

A separate VC partner program lets firms bring their portfolios in. As I see it, that makes the VC and accelerator relationship the distribution layer: one good partner can deliver dozens of founders who are choosing their model provider right now.

This role owns that layer as an individual contributor with high autonomy, working across sales, marketing and product. It is a 0-to-1 program role that has to scale on data: offerings, accelerator partnerships, events and promotions, founder feedback into the roadmap, and the metrics that show what is working.

Startups pick a model early and tend to stay with it. Anthropic needs a repeatable way to reach founders through the VCs and accelerators they trust, and to prove which programs actually move portfolio companies to build and spend on Claude.
