05The first 90 daysBrand and P&L together
IListen and measure IITest the new story IIIScale and harvest
I
Days 1 to 30

Listen and measure

Protect the engine first

I would learn the business from the people who built it, then read every number.

  • Time with ownership, the Head of Innovation and every team member.
  • Audit DR, broadcast and digital spend on blended ROAS, CAC and contribution margin.
  • Read customer reviews and the stories families choose to keep.
  • Map the team and agency mix. Recommend the email ownership model.
  • Weekly channel scorecard live by week four.
The rule

Change nothing that pays until the numbers are clear.

II
Days 31 to 60

Test the new story

Let customers vote with money

The identity story gets written in the family historian's voice, then tested against today's best ads.

  • Draft the brand reframe and brand standards with ownership.
  • New paid social creative tested against the current control, on the same scorecard.
  • Creator, UGC, PR and influencer pilots with clear economics.
  • Rebuild the core lifecycle flows.
  • Brief the flagship site redesign around conversion and trust.
The rule

A premium story has to win in the ad account too.

III
Days 61 to 90

Scale and harvest

One story across every channel

Winning messages roll out across DR, broadcast and digital, with one voice and one set of numbers.

  • Holiday harvest-season calendar, built on what tested best.
  • First LTV model, shared with ownership.
  • Hiring plan for the gaps: design, PR, influencer, senior brand.
  • Next-year plan for brand and performance, in one deck.
The rule

Brand and performance answer to the same scorecard.
