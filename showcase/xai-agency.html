01 · xAI

What I see you building

xAI, now SpaceXAI, runs X's advertising business and is adding ads to Grok. Rebuilding advertiser spend runs largely through agencies, and independent and performance shops are the ones closest to measurable results.

This senior IC role, framed internally as Head of Independent Agencies, owns that portfolio. The job is to grow agency-driven revenue, bring in new advertisers, train agencies on products, brand safety and measurement, and feed their insights back to Product and Marketing.

It also manages third-party measurement partners and joint go-to-market, and coordinates with holding-company and Big 6 agency leads where coverage overlaps. The role is subject to ITAR.

Independent and performance agencies decide on proof and margin, and X needs someone who speaks their language to turn them into growing, measurable spend.
