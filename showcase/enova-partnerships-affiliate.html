Chapter 1

What I see at Enova

A Chicago fintech that lends to people and small businesses traditional banks underserve, with an AI and machine-learning engine that decides fast. The partnerships seat sits right where growth, risk and regulation meet.

Key figures, fiscal year 2025
MeasureReported
Total revenue, up 19% year on year$3.2B
Marketing expense (2024: $523.6M)$621.1M

Customers served since founding14M+
Source: Enova fourth quarter and full year 2025 results, January 27, 2026.
  • Two consumer brands, two customer needs.CashNetUSA and NetCredit serve non-prime consumers; OnDeck serves small businesses. This role grows the consumer side, so every partner has to fit the right brand and the right borrower.
  • A bank is coming into the house.Enova agreed to buy Grasshopper Bank in December 2025 for about $369M, with closing expected in the second half of 2026. Partner programs will need to work cleanly with bank partners and regulators.
  • Marketing is a growth engine, and it is growing.Marketing spend rose about 19% in 2025, in step with revenue. At that scale, a few points of partner efficiency are worth real money.
  • Colossus decides; partners feed it.Enova's machine-learning platform underwrites in real time. The best partners are the ones whose traffic it approves and that customers repay, not the ones who send the most applications.

Chapter 2

The brief

What Enova is asking for

  • Run a portfolio of 20+ strategic and affiliate partnerships
  • Track partner performance weekly, then optimize and scale
  • Lead business development and contract negotiation
  • Work with Legal, Compliance and Bank Partnerships
  • Turn BI data into decisions for senior leaders
  • Lead 1 to 2 direct reports, and use AI in the work

What Enova is offering

$150K to $190K base

Plus bonus and RSUs. Hybrid in Chicago, in office Tuesday to Thursday. Medical, dental and vision, 401(k) match, sabbatical program, summer hours and paid parental leave.

The problem to solve

Grow customer acquisition through partners without growing risk. That means finding the partners whose customers get approved and repay, paying them on what matters, and keeping every consumer touchpoint compliant as Enova becomes a bank holding company.

Outlook: the first 90 days

Three outlook quarters, one per month of the first 90 days, each with a clear deliverable. Listen first, then fix, then grow.

Line Q1Days 1 to 30: understand Q2Days 31 to 60: tune Q3Days 61 to 90: grow
Portfolio
  • Meet all 20+ partners and both direct reports
  • Rank each partner on approval rate, funded loans, early repayment and cost per funded loan
  • Move budget toward partners whose customers Colossus approves and who repay
  • Put the bottom tier on a clear improvement plan
  • Sign 2 to 3 new partners in gaps the scorecard shows
  • Pilot one co-marketing program per brand
Data and AI
  • Agree with BI on one weekly partner scorecard
  • Map which partner data already exists
  • Automate the weekly read-out with AI-drafted summaries the team checks
  • Monthly partner review for marketing leadership, with spend and outcome by partner
Compliance
  • Sit with Legal, Compliance and Bank Partnerships to learn the rules partners must follow
  • Add consent, disclosure and audit terms to the standard partner contract
  • Plan partner readiness for the Grasshopper Bank integration
Deliverable Partner scorecard and a written read on the portfolio Rebalanced spend and updated contract terms A 12-month partner growth plan with targets
