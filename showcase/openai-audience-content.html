01 · OpenAI

What I see you building

ChatGPT Ads launched in 2026 and passed a $1 billion annualized run rate in under 200 days. Self-serve Ads Manager opened more broadly in May and now reaches tens of thousands of advertisers in more than 40 countries. Small and midsized businesses are already a meaningful share of that base.

The SMB Ads Marketing team decides how those businesses discover the platform, succeed as advertisers and grow their spend, working with Product, Ads Engineering, Data Science and Sales.

This lead builds the content and education side from zero: editorial strategy, webinars, video series, case studies, vertical playbooks and creator-led programs. The role owns both production and distribution, adapted for regional markets.

Most small businesses have never bought ads inside a conversational AI product, and OpenAI needs education and content that make the new channel clear enough for them to launch, learn and increase spend.
