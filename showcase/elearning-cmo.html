How I'd be measured.

Your goal is to grow from about $20MM to $50MM+. These are the numbers I would put on the board in week one and report to the CEO and COO every week after.

M1

ROAS by channel and blended

Every dollar of paid spend tied to first-purchase revenue and to 90-day revenue, so we scale what pays back.

M2

Cost per new member

Acquisition cost for each new member or student, split by channel, offer and partner.

M3

Net membership growth

New members minus churn, tracked monthly, with retention by cohort and by entry offer.

M4

Revenue per webinar registrant

Show rate, offer rate and sales per registrant for every launch and evergreen webinar or VSL.

M5

Partner share of new revenue

The part of new revenue that comes from affiliates, JV authors and creators, paid only when they produce.

M6

Ascension to high-ticket

How many entry buyers move into the higher-ticket programs, and the lifetime value that follows.

The first 90 days.

A plan for a course, coaching and membership business. Audit first, then build the engines, then scale what the numbers back.

  1. Days 1 to 30

    Audit the offer and the funnel

    Find where the money leaks.

    Map every path from first click to high-ticket enrollment, with real numbers at each step.

    • Offer and funnel audit: entry offers, price points, upsells and the ascension path
    • Channel review with the team: ROAS, cost per member and payback by source
    • Membership churn by cohort; first fixes to onboarding and the first 30 days
    • Lead sourcing and consent review for compliance

  2. Days 31 to 60

    Build the engines

    Webinars, email and partners.

    Stand up the assets and programs that compound, with a test plan for each.

    • Webinar and VSL refresh, with new email sequences for registrants, no-shows and buyers
    • Affiliate and JV partner program with other authors and coaches, with clear tiers and payouts
    • Creator and UGC program for paid social creative and organic reach
    • AI content workflow to raise creative and copy velocity for the team

  3. Days 61 to 90

    Scale what pays back

    Put budget behind proven numbers.

    Shift spend toward the channels and partners that clear their targets, and cut what does not.

    • Scale winning paid campaigns and partner launches against ROAS targets
    • Membership retention program: milestones, community moments and win-back offers
    • Weekly scorecard for the CEO and COO on the six measures
    • A 12-month growth plan tied to the $50MM+ goal
