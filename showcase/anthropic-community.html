01 · Anthropic

What I see you building

Claude now reaches three very different audiences at once: developers building on the API and Claude Code, individual knowledge workers using Claude every day, and enterprise buyers rolling it out across whole organizations. Anthropic runs both a self-serve motion and an enterprise sales motion, and the two feed each other.

This role sits in Enterprise Marketing but owns community globally. The job is to take the programs that already exist (events, ambassadors, training, certification, support) and run them as one function that helps millions of people build real skills with Claude, reported on retained users and product adoption rather than attendance.

The center of gravity is the enterprise account: finding the power users inside a customer, turning them into champions and trainers, and working with Sales and Customer Success so that one team's enthusiasm becomes company-wide usage.

Anthropic has strong community energy but needs it run as a single operating model that measurably moves users from non-user to high-value daily user and turns self-serve adoption into enterprise expansion.
