01 · Databricks

What I see you building

Databricks sells the Data Intelligence Platform, a consumption-based business where customers pay as they use data, analytics and AI workloads. Its newer products center on building AI agents on top of a company's own data, so the company has a clear stake in showing what agent-driven work looks like.

This is a new team reporting to the AVP of Marketing Technology, Data, and Growth. It owns annual marketing planning and a single planning system of record, a capacity model that counts staff, agencies and AI agents, and the build-out of the highest-value marketing workflows for agent execution.

The second half of the job is adoption: setting standards for prompts, context and agent workflows across a global, matrixed marketing organization, with governance for quality, risk and privacy, so the change lasts past the first rollout.

Databricks marketing needs to plan and allocate spend with explicit trade-offs tied to pipeline, and to redesign how its work gets done so AI agents cut time to market across a large global team.
