The problem to solve

Every new condition is a new launch: a new audience, new claims rules and a new channel mix.

Without a system, each one starts from zero. With one, the fifth condition launches faster and cheaper than the first, because the audience, the creative approvals, the partners and the measurement already exist.

The job is to build that system while the first two conditions are still finding their footing.

A 90-day care plan for Patiently

Assess, treat and sustain: understand the two live conditions, build the launch playbook on them, then prove it on the next one.

Days 1–30Assess

Learn the patient path

Know exactly where weight loss and menopause patients start, stall and stay.

  • Sit down with the CMO, Chief Digital Health Officer, Clinical, Compliance and Sales
  • Map each funnel: visit, intake start, intake complete, provider approval, first shipment, month-three refill
  • Build unit economics per condition and channel: CPA, LTV, payback
  • Inventory the owned audience: FSA Store and HSA Store customers, email, SMS and eligible-product buyers
  • Agree a claims review process with Compliance and Legal

Outcome: one shared scorecard for both conditions.

Days 31–60Treat

Build the launch playbook

Turn what's working into a repeatable system.

  • Playbook v1: positioning, an approved creative and claims library, landing pages, offer and KPI gates
  • Cross-sell from the stores to shoppers whose purchases signal a fit for each condition
  • Open a compliant partner channel with vetted publishers and creators on clear payouts
  • Pilot B2B2C with benefits administrators and employers alongside Sales
  • Hire or assign owners for acquisition, CRM and partnerships

Outcome: a documented playbook and partner program live.

Days 61–90Sustain

Prove it on the next condition

Show the system makes launch three faster and cheaper.

  • Run the next condition launch through the playbook with leadership
  • Set the budget model by condition and channel against payback targets
  • Ship an executive dashboard on acquisition, retention and ROI
  • Lock in the team structure and agency roster for the next year

Outcome: a launch calendar leadership can fund with confidence.
