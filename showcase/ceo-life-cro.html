01 / What I see at CEO Life04

I know your community, and your member.

The business

A private community for CEOs, founders and senior executives, built chapter by chapter.

Since 2022 CEO Life has grown to 35+ chapters with monthly dinners, quarterly peer roundtables and a national gathering. The experience is proven. Now it has to replicate.

The member

Established CEOs and founders who buy access to peers, not content.

I'm one of them: a founder who has built and run companies for 20+ years. I know what makes a busy CEO say yes to a room, and what makes them stay.

The market

A crowded field of peer groups, masterminds and executive forums.

Members choose on who else is in the room. That makes referrals, partners and member-get-member the cheapest and best channels, with paid feeding the top.

The model

Chapter memberships plus high-ticket Chapter Partnerships, targeting 150+ chapters.

Two sales motions with one engine: a membership funnel each chapter can run, and a partnership sale that recruits the people who run chapters.

My thesis for CEO Life

CEO Life grows when every member becomes a recruiter and every chapter runs the same playbook with the same scorecard.
