01 / What I see at Credible04

I know your marketplace, and your borrower.

The heritage

The category Credible was built on.

Credible made its name helping borrowers compare student loan rates from many lenders in one place. The brand trust in this category is the company's oldest asset.

The customer

Students, parents and graduates making one of their first big money decisions.

They're anxious, they're comparing, and they want an honest answer. Being the friend you wish you had in finance matters most right here.

The market

Seasonal, rate-sensitive and crowded with lenders and comparison sites.

Demand spikes around the school calendar and moves with rates. The GM who plans for the season, and keeps lenders bidding, wins it.

The model

Lender partners on one side, borrowers on the other.

Revenue per borrower minus cost per borrower. I've managed that spread on lead marketplaces for two decades.

My thesis for Credible

Student loans don't need a louder ad. They need a better spread: more lenders bidding, cheaper borrowers from trusted partners, and a season planned months ahead.
