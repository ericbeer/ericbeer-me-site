01 · ElevenLabs

What I see you building

ElevenLabs started with the most natural-sounding AI voices and has grown into a full conversational agent platform used by developers and enterprises. In September 2026 it launched Reception, a self-serve AI receptionist that answers calls and books appointments for small businesses, priced from $29 a month.

Reception is ElevenLabs' route below its enterprise voice-agent platform: no sales call, no integration project, just a business owner signing up and turning it on. That makes growth a self-serve funnel problem, from signup and onboarding through activation and conversion to paid.

This role owns that growth: building channels, finding what drives activation and retention among SMBs, and feeding what it learns back into the product roadmap.

Reception needs a repeatable way to reach busy owners in home services, healthcare, hospitality and professional services, get them to their first answered call quickly, and convert them to paid at a cost that works on a low monthly price.
